# Cardiometabolic Service Line Strategy Brief 2026

## 1. Heart Failure Program Position

Riverbend Health admitted 2,230 heart failure patients in the last four quarters. The GDMT four-pillar rate at discharge is 41%, well below the 80% benchmark, and the 30-day readmission rate is 21.4% after a peak of 24.8%. Competitor St. Brendan Regional opened a dedicated HF titration clinic in 2025 and is recruiting for two industry trials.

## 3. Diabetes Program Position

The primary-care diabetes panel is 38,400 adults; 58% have an A1C below 8%, short of the 70% target. CGM use outside insulin users is 12%. A remission pathway and pharmacist-led titration are the lowest-cost levers.

## 4. Recommendations for Leadership

- Fund a pharmacist-led GDMT titration clinic in FY27 (target four-pillar rate of 65% within 12 months).

- Apply to be a site for two late-stage HF trials to strengthen academic positioning.

- Pilot CGM-linked coaching for non-insulin patients with an A1C above 9%.
