# Measuring Equipment Calibration and Gauge R&R

## Calibration Intervals
- Calipers and micrometers: every 6 months.
- Height gauges, bore gauges and indicators: every 12 months.
- Thread plug and ring gauges: every 12 months, or after 5,000 uses.
- Torque wrenches: every 6 months or 5,000 cycles, whichever comes first.
- CMM: annual calibration by an accredited (ISO/IEC 17025) provider, plus a daily probe qualification.

## Calibration Status Labels
Every gauge carries a label with the gauge ID, calibration date and due date. A gauge with a missing or expired label must not be used; return it to the Quality lab.

## Out-of-Tolerance Gauge Found
1. Remove the gauge from use and tag it DO NOT USE.
2. Notify Quality within the shift.
3. Quality performs a product impact assessment: all lots measured with that gauge since the last successful calibration are identified.
4. Affected lots still on site are placed on HOLD and re-inspected with a calibrated gauge; shipped lots are evaluated for customer notification.

## Gauge R&R Acceptance Criteria
Measurement systems used for CTQ characteristics require a Gauge R&R study (10 parts, 3 operators, 3 trials):
- %GRR below 10%: acceptable.
- %GRR 10-30%: conditionally acceptable with Quality Engineer approval, based on the application.
- %GRR above 30%: not acceptable; improve the measurement system.
- Number of distinct categories (ndc) must be 5 or more.
