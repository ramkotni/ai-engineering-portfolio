# Nonconforming Product and Corrective Action (CAPA)

## Identifying Nonconforming Product
Any part that does not meet drawing, specification or visual standard requirements is nonconforming. When found:
1. Stop and segregate the suspect parts immediately.
2. Attach a red REJECT tag (for confirmed defects) or a yellow HOLD tag (for suspect parts awaiting inspection).
3. Move tagged parts to the locked MRB cage within the same shift.
4. Open a Nonconformance Report (NCR) in the MES within 4 hours, including part number, lot, quantity, defect description and photos.

## Material Review Board (MRB) Disposition
The MRB (Quality Engineer, Production Supervisor, Manufacturing Engineer) reviews NCRs within 2 working days. Allowed dispositions:
- Rework: return to conforming condition using an approved rework instruction; re-inspect 100%.
- Repair: usable but not fully conforming; requires customer approval.
- Use-as-is: requires Quality Engineer approval and, for customer-owned characteristics, written customer deviation.
- Scrap: mark with red paint and record in the scrap log.
- Return to supplier: for purchased material defects; Purchasing issues a supplier corrective action request (SCAR).
Supervisors may never ship, rework or scrap nonconforming product without an MRB disposition.

## When a CAPA Is Required
Open a Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) when:
- A defect reaches a customer (customer complaint).
- The same defect repeats 3 or more times within 30 days.
- Cpk on a CTQ characteristic falls below 1.33 (see QCS-002).
- An internal audit finding is classified as major.

## CAPA Process (8D)
D1 team, D2 problem description, D3 containment within 24 hours, D4 root cause (5-Why and fishbone), D5 permanent corrective action, D6 implementation and verification, D7 prevent recurrence (update PFMEA, control plan and work instructions), D8 closure. Target closure is 30 days; effectiveness is verified after 90 days.

## Escalation
Contact the Quality Engineer (ext. 4420) for any suspected escape to a customer or any safety-critical characteristic.
