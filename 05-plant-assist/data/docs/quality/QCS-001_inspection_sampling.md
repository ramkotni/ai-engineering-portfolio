# Inspection and Sampling Standard (Incoming, In-Process, Final)

## First Article Inspection (FAI)
A first article inspection is required for every new part number, every drawing revision change, a new fixture or program, and after any machine crash or major repair. The FAI checks 100% of drawing characteristics on the first good part and is recorded on form QF-210. Production may not run until the FAI is approved by Quality.

## Incoming Inspection
Purchased material is sampled per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, general inspection level II, single sampling, normal inspection:
- Critical characteristics: AQL 0.65
- Major characteristics: AQL 1.0
- Minor characteristics: AQL 2.5
Example: a lot of 500 pieces requires a sample of 50 (code letter H); for AQL 1.0 accept on 1 defect, reject on 2.

## In-Process Inspection
- Operators measure the first piece after every setup, tool change or offset change.
- Sampling frequency during the run: 5 consecutive parts every 2 hours, recorded on the SPC chart (see QCS-002).
- Critical-to-quality (CTQ) dimensions marked with a diamond symbol on the drawing are checked 100%.

## Final Inspection
- Visual inspection of 100% of parts for burrs, scratches and dents per visual standard VS-03.
- Dimensional audit of 13 parts per lot (c=0, accept on zero defects).
- Final inspection results and lot traceability are recorded in the MES before shipping.

## Measurement Rules
- Use only gauges with a valid calibration sticker (see QCS-004).
- Gauge resolution must be at most 1/10 of the tolerance band.
- Parts are measured at room temperature, 20 C +/- 2 C, for tolerances tighter than 0.001 in.
