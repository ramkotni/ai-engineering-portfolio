# AC-75 Rotary Screw Air Compressor - Maintenance Manual

## Overview
The AC-75 is a 75 hp (55 kW) oil-flooded rotary screw compressor supplying plant air at 110 psi, with a refrigerated dryer and a 1,060-gallon receiver tank. Before maintenance, apply LOTO per SOP-SAF-001, close the discharge isolation valve and vent the compressor and separator tank to 0 psi. The compressor can auto-restart; never rely on the controller STOP button.

## Maintenance Intervals
- Daily: record discharge pressure, discharge temperature (normal 80-95 C) and dryer dew point (target 3 C / 38 F); drain receiver condensate if the auto-drain is faulty.
- Every 2,000 hours: replace the air filter element (part AF-75) and the oil filter (part OF-75).
- Every 4,000 hours: replace the separator element (part SE-75).
- Every 8,000 hours or 2 years: change the compressor fluid (8 gal synthetic compressor fluid), and check the safety relief valve.

## Checking Compressor Oil Level
Check the oil level only with the compressor stopped for at least 5 minutes and the sump depressurized. The level must be in the green band of the sight glass.

## Troubleshooting
- High discharge temperature shutdown (above 110 C): clean the cooler, check the oil level, check the thermal valve.
- Low plant air pressure: check for leaks with ultrasound, check the inlet valve and the air filter differential pressure.
- Oil carryover into the air lines: replace the separator element and check the scavenge line orifice.
