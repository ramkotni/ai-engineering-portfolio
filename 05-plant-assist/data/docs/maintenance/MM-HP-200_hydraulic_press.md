# HP-200 200-Ton Hydraulic Press - Maintenance Manual

## Machine Overview
The HP-200 is a 200-ton four-post hydraulic press with a 30 kW pump motor, a 120-gallon reservoir and a nitrogen-charged accumulator. Normal system operating pressure is 2,800 psi; the main relief valve is set to 3,000 psi. Before any maintenance task, apply lockout/tagout per SOP-SAF-001, bleed the accumulator to 0 psi and lower the ram onto the safety blocks.

## Preventive Maintenance Schedule
- Daily (operator): check oil level in the sight glass (must be between MIN and MAX), check for leaks, verify light curtain function with the test rod.
- Weekly: inspect hoses and fittings for abrasion or weeping; clean the oil cooler fins.
- Monthly: check accumulator nitrogen pre-charge (target 1,400 psi +/- 50 psi with the system at 0 psi); lubricate the four guide posts with EP-2 grease.
- Every 1,000 operating hours: replace the return-line filter element (part no. HF-10-25, 10 micron) and take an oil sample.
- Every 4,000 operating hours or annually: change the hydraulic oil (ISO VG 46, 120 gal) and the suction strainer.

## Hydraulic Oil Specification
Use only ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil. Target cleanliness is ISO 4406 code 17/15/12 or better. Normal operating oil temperature is 40-55 C; the press alarms at 65 C and shuts down at 70 C.

## Replacing the Main Cylinder Seal Kit
Required parts: seal kit SK-200-MC. Required tools: seal pick set, torque wrench (up to 400 ft-lb), clean lint-free cloths.
1. Apply LOTO per SOP-SAF-001; bleed the accumulator to 0 psi and lower the ram onto the safety blocks.
2. Disconnect and cap the cylinder hydraulic lines.
3. Remove the gland retainer bolts and withdraw the gland.
4. Remove the old seals with the seal pick; do not scratch the seal grooves.
5. Clean the grooves and lubricate the new seals with clean ISO VG 46 oil before installation.
6. Reinstall the gland and torque the retainer bolts to 210 ft-lb in a star pattern.
7. Restore the lines, remove LOTO per SOP-SAF-001, then cycle the press 10 times at low pressure (500 psi) and inspect for leaks before returning to production.

## Troubleshooting
- Ram drifts down when stopped: check the counterbalance valve setting and the main cylinder seals.
- Slow approach speed: check the prefill valve and the pump compensator setting.
- Oil overheating: clean the cooler fins, verify cooling water flow (minimum 8 gpm), check the oil level.
- Excessive pump noise: check for cavitation from a clogged suction strainer or low oil level.

## Escalation
Contact the Maintenance Planner (ext. 4410) for parts not stocked in the crib or when the troubleshooting steps do not resolve the problem.
