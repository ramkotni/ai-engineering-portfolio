# CV-3 Belt Conveyor Line - Maintenance Manual

## Equipment Overview
The CV-3 line is a 60 m belt conveyor driven by a 5.5 kW gearmotor at 0.5 m/s, with pull-cord emergency stops along its full length. Pull cords must be tested weekly. All work inside guards requires lockout/tagout per SOP-SAF-001 - stopping with the pull cord is NOT a lockout.

## Clearing a Jam
1. Stop the conveyor and apply LOTO per SOP-SAF-001 at disconnect DS-CV3.
2. Verify zero energy by pressing START at the local station.
3. Remove the jammed parts using a hook tool; never reach into a nip point.
4. Inspect the belt and side guides for damage.
5. Reinstall guards, remove LOTO and restart.

## Belt Tracking Adjustment
Belt tracking is adjusted at the tail pulley take-up screws with the belt running at the slowest speed and the guards in place, using the external adjustment points only. Turn the take-up screw on the side the belt is running toward by a quarter turn, then wait for three full belt revolutions before adjusting again.

## Belt Tension
Correct tension is achieved when the belt deflects 15-20 mm under a 10 kg load at mid-span between rollers on the return side. Over-tensioning shortens bearing life.

## Lubrication Schedule
- Gearbox: check oil weekly through the sight glass; change synthetic ISO VG 220 gear oil every 5,000 hours.
- Pulley bearings: grease monthly with 2 pumps of NLGI 2 lithium complex grease.
- Drive chain: lubricate weekly with chain oil.

## Troubleshooting
- Belt slipping on the drive pulley: increase tension or check for pulley lagging wear.
- Belt mistracking: check for material build-up on rollers, then adjust tracking.
- Motor tripping on overload: check for jams, seized rollers or excessive belt tension.

## Escalation
Contact the Maintenance Planner (ext. 4410) for belt splicing or gearmotor replacement.
