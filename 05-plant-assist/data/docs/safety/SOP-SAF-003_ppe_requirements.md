# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements

## Plant-Wide Minimum PPE
All people on the production floor, including visitors, must wear:
- ANSI Z87.1 safety glasses with side shields
- Steel- or composite-toe safety shoes (ASTM F2413)
- Hearing protection in areas marked as high noise (85 dBA or above)
- High-visibility vest in forklift traffic aisles

## Task-Specific PPE Matrix
| Task / Area | Required PPE | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CNC machining (VMC-500) | Safety glasses, hearing protection | No gloves near the rotating spindle; cut-resistant gloves (ANSI A4) only when handling sharp parts with the spindle stopped |
| Hydraulic press (HP-200) operation | Safety glasses, cut-resistant gloves (ANSI A4), hearing protection | Gloves for blank handling |
| Grinding / deburring | Face shield over safety glasses, leather gloves, hearing protection | |
| Chemical handling (coolant, solvent) | Nitrile gloves, chemical splash goggles | |
| Sodium hydroxide parts washer | Chemical splash goggles, face shield, chemical apron, neoprene gloves | Corrosive - see SOP-SAF-002 |
| Electrical work above 50 V | Arc-rated clothing per arc-flash label, voltage-rated gloves, arc-rated face shield | |
| Working at heights above 4 ft | Full-body harness, 6 ft shock-absorbing lanyard | Tie off to an approved anchor |

## Rotating Equipment Rule
Gloves, loose clothing, jewelry and long hair (unless secured) are prohibited within 2 ft of rotating spindles, chucks, conveyors and shafts due to entanglement hazard.

## Inspection and Replacement
- Inspect PPE before each use. Replace cracked glasses, damaged gloves and harnesses that have arrested a fall.
- Harnesses are inspected monthly by a competent person and tagged.
- Replacement PPE is issued from the tool crib with supervisor approval.

## Enforcement
Supervisors must stop work when required PPE is missing. Repeat violations are reported to the EHS Manager (ext. 4400).
