# Emergency Response - Fire, Injury and Evacuation

## Emergency Contacts
- Plant emergency line: **ext. 5555** (mobile: 555-0100) - staffed 24/7 by security
- External emergency services: 911
- EHS Manager: ext. 4400
- First-aid responders are identified by a green cross on their hard hat.

## Fire
1. Activate the nearest red fire alarm pull station.
2. Call ext. 5555 and give the exact location.
3. Only trained employees may use a fire extinguisher, and only on a small fire (smaller than a wastebasket) with a clear exit behind them. Use the PASS method: Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep.
4. For fires in electrical panels use only CO2 or ABC extinguishers - never water.
5. If the fire cannot be controlled within 30 seconds, evacuate.

## Evacuation
- Leave by the nearest marked exit; do not use freight elevators.
- Supervisors sweep their area, then report to the assembly point (Parking Lot B, north side) with the shift roster.
- Do not re-enter the building until the Incident Commander gives the all-clear.

## Injury Response
1. Make sure the scene is safe; stop the machine with the emergency stop button if needed.
2. Call ext. 5555 for any serious injury (bleeding that will not stop, amputation, burn, loss of consciousness, crush injury).
3. Do not move an injured person unless they are in immediate danger.
4. A first-aid responder provides care until EMS arrives.
5. Preserve the scene; the machine stays locked out until the EHS investigation releases it.
6. The supervisor completes incident report EHS-101 within 4 hours.

## Near Misses
All near misses must be reported in the EHS portal by the end of the shift. Near-miss reporting is blame-free.
