# Chemical Handling and Spill Response

## Chemicals in Use
Common chemicals on the floor: water-soluble metalworking coolant concentrate (CoolCut 40), hydraulic oil ISO VG 46, degreaser solvent (SolvClean 7, flammable), and 10% sodium hydroxide parts-wash solution (corrosive). Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are in the yellow binder at each department station and in the online SDS portal.

## Handling Requirements
- Read the SDS before first use of any chemical.
- Store flammables (SolvClean 7) only in the yellow flammable cabinet; maximum 25 gallons outside the cabinet per work area.
- Secondary containers must have a GHS label showing product name and hazard pictograms.
- Coolant concentrate is mixed at the central mixing station only, at a 6-8% concentration.
- Never mix sodium hydroxide solution with acids or with aluminum parts.

## Spill Classification
- **Minor spill**: less than 5 gallons, known chemical, no injury, not entering a drain. Can be cleaned by trained area personnel.
- **Major spill**: 5 gallons or more, unknown chemical, any injury, fire risk, or any release to a floor drain or outside. Requires the Emergency Response Team.

## Minor Spill Response
1. Alert people nearby and restrict access to the area.
2. Put on PPE per SDS: nitrile gloves, chemical splash goggles; add a face shield and chemical apron for sodium hydroxide.
3. Stop the source if it is safe to do so.
4. Contain using the spill kit (yellow drum): place absorbent socks around the spill, then pads/granular absorbent on top.
5. Collect used absorbent into the labeled waste bag and drum; contact EHS for waste pickup.
6. Report the spill to the supervisor and log it in the EHS portal within 24 hours.

## Major Spill Response
1. Evacuate the immediate area and move upwind.
2. Call plant emergency **ext. 5555** (from mobile: 555-0100) and state the location, chemical and approximate quantity.
3. Shut off ignition sources only if it can be done without risk.
4. Do not attempt cleanup. Wait for the Emergency Response Team at a safe distance and provide the SDS.

## First Aid for Chemical Exposure
- Eyes: flush at the nearest eyewash station for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open.
- Skin: remove contaminated clothing and rinse under the safety shower for at least 15 minutes.
- Seek medical evaluation and report every exposure to the supervisor.
