PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Northwind Robotics designs, manufactures and services collaborative robots (cobots), autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and the FleetOS software that orchestrates them in factories and warehouses. We sell to automotive, electronics, logistics and food-and-beverage manufacturers in 31 countries.

We report two segments: Robotics Systems (cobots and AMRs, 78% of 2025 revenue) and Software and Services (FleetOS subscriptions, maintenance and integration, 22% of 2025 revenue). FleetOS annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached $212 million at December 31, 2025.

Our three largest customers, all automotive manufacturers, accounted for 31% of 2025 revenue. Revenue outside the United States was 38% of the total. We employ 4,120 people and manufacture in Columbus, Ohio and Monterrey, Mexico.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Investing in our common stock involves risks. The risks below could materially affect our business.

Customer concentration. Our three largest customers accounted for 31% of revenue in 2025. A delay or cancellation of an automotive retooling program could reduce revenue materially.

Tariffs and trade policy. Harmonic-drive actuators and controllers are imported from suppliers in Asia. New or higher tariffs would increase cost of revenue; we estimate each 10 percentage points of tariff on these components reduces gross margin by about 60 basis points.

Competition. Low-cost cobot manufacturers continue to enter the market, and large industrial-automation companies bundle robots with their control systems, which may pressure our pricing.

Semiconductor supply. We rely on a single source for the safety-rated motion controller used in all cobot models.

Cybersecurity of connected fleets. FleetOS connects customer robots to our cloud. A security incident could disrupt customer operations and expose us to liability.

Foreign currency. 38% of revenue is denominated in currencies other than the U.S. dollar, mainly the euro and the Mexican peso.

PART II

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Revenue grew 13.6% to $1,541.3 million in 2025, driven by AMR shipments (+24%) and FleetOS subscriptions (+31%). Robotics Systems revenue grew 9.8% as automotive retooling slowed in the second half.

Gross margin expanded 100 basis points to 45.0% on a higher software mix and lower freight costs. Operating income rose to $161.8 million (10.5% operating margin) from $130.3 million.

Research and development expense increased to $184.9 million (12.0% of revenue), reflecting the next-generation AMR platform.

Liquidity: cash and cash equivalents were $412.6 million and total debt was $350.0 million at year end. We repaid $35.0 million of term debt in 2025.

Outlook: for 2026 we expect revenue growth of 11% to 13% and a modest improvement in operating margin.
