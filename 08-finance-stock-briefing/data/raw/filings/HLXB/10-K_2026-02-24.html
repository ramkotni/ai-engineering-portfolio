PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Helix Biologics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing biologic and genetic medicines for cardiometabolic disease. Our revenue consists of royalties and collaboration revenue from partnered programs; we have no product approved for sale.

Our lead program is HLX-INS7, a once-weekly basal insulin in Phase 3 (study SYN-DM-203) for type 2 diabetes. Other programs include HLX-SERCA, an AAV gene therapy for heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (Phase 2, SYN-HF-103), HLX-207, an anti-fibrotic antibody (Phase 1, SYN-HF-109), and HLX-AMY, an amylin co-agonist (Phase 1, SYN-DM-210).

We employ 860 people and operate a clinical-scale AAV manufacturing facility in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Investing in our common stock involves risks. The risks below could materially affect our business.

Clinical development risk. HLX-INS7 may fail to meet its endpoints or regulators may require additional studies before approval.

Dependence on HLX-INS7. A substantial part of our valuation depends on HLX-INS7; a setback would materially affect our prospects.

Gene therapy manufacturing. AAV vector manufacturing is complex and capacity-constrained; batch failures could delay HLX-SERCA.

Competition. Large insulin manufacturers market daily and weekly basal insulins with established payer contracts.

Need for additional capital. We had $1,105.3 million of cash and investments at year end and expect it to fund operations into 2028. We may raise additional equity, which would dilute shareholders.

Pricing and reimbursement. Government price negotiation and payer formulary decisions could limit the price of future products.

PART II

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Revenue increased 13.9% to $191.4 million in 2025, reflecting higher royalties on a partnered product and a $12.0 million collaboration milestone.

Research and development expense increased to $412.7 million from $372.8 million, driven by Phase 3 enrollment for HLX-INS7 and AAV manufacturing scale-up for HLX-SERCA.

Net loss was $298.6 million, or $3.41 per diluted share, compared with $270.9 million, or $3.15 per diluted share, in 2024.

Liquidity: cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $1,105.3 million at December 31, 2025. We have no debt.

Outlook: we expect topline Phase 3 results for HLX-INS7 in the second half of 2026 and a biologics license application in 2027 if positive.
