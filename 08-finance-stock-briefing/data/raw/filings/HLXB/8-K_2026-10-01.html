Helix Biologics Corp. (Nasdaq: HLXB)

SYNTHETIC FILING - FICTIONAL COMPANY

Item 8.01 Other Events

Helix Biologics announced topline results from the Phase 3 study SYN-DM-203 of HLX-INS7, a once-weekly basal insulin, in 1,500 adults with type 2 diabetes. HLX-INS7 met the primary endpoint of non-inferior A1C reduction at 52 weeks (-1.42 percentage points versus -1.31 for once-daily basal insulin). Rates of clinically significant hypoglycemia were similar between groups. The company plans a biologics license application in the first quarter of 2027.
