# Pharmacy Benefits and Formulary

## Drug Tiers and Copays (30-day retail supply)
- Tier 1 - Generic drugs: $10 copay.
- Tier 2 - Preferred brand drugs: $40 copay.
- Tier 3 - Non-preferred brand drugs: $80 copay.
- Tier 4 - Specialty drugs: 25% coinsurance up to a maximum of $250 per prescription.

## Mail Order Pharmacy
Members can receive a 90-day supply of maintenance medications through Evergreen Mail Order for 2 times the 30-day retail copay (for example, a Tier 1 generic 90-day supply costs $20).

## Step Therapy
Some medications require step therapy, meaning the member must first try a lower-cost, clinically appropriate alternative before the plan covers the higher-cost drug.

## Specialty Drugs and GLP-1 Medications
All Tier 4 specialty drugs and all GLP-1 medications require prior authorization and must be filled through the Evergreen Specialty Pharmacy.

## Formulary Exceptions
If a medication is not on the formulary, the prescriber may submit a formulary exception request. Standard exception requests are decided within 72 hours; expedited requests within 24 hours.
