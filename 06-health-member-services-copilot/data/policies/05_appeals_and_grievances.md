# Appeals and Grievances

## Internal Appeals
If a claim or prior authorization is denied, the member (or their authorized representative) may file an internal appeal. The appeal must be filed within 180 days of the date on the denial notice.

## How to File an Appeal
Appeals can be submitted:
- Online through the Evergreen member portal (Claims > Appeal a Decision).
- By fax to 1-800-555-0199.
- By mail to Evergreen Health Plans, Appeals Unit, PO Box 4410, Portland, OR 97208.

Include the claim number, the denial letter or EOB, a written explanation of why you disagree, and any supporting medical records or letters from your provider.

## Appeal Decision Timelines
- Pre-service appeals (care not yet received): decided within 30 calendar days.
- Post-service appeals (care already received): decided within 60 calendar days.
- Expedited appeals for urgent situations: decided within 72 hours.

## External Review
If the internal appeal is denied, the member may request an external review by an Independent Review Organization (IRO). The external review request must be filed within 4 months of the final internal appeal decision. The IRO decision is binding on Evergreen.

## Grievances
A grievance is a complaint about quality of service, wait times, or provider behavior (not a coverage decision). Grievances can be filed by phone or through the portal and are resolved within 30 days.
