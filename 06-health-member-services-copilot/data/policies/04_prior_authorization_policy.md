# Prior Authorization Policy

## What Is Prior Authorization
Prior authorization (also called pre-approval) is a review Evergreen performs before certain services are provided to confirm they are medically necessary and covered. The ordering provider is responsible for requesting prior authorization.

## Services That Require Prior Authorization
- Advanced imaging: MRI, CT, and PET scans.
- Non-emergency inpatient hospital admissions.
- Outpatient surgery.
- Specialty drugs, including all GLP-1 medications.
- Durable medical equipment (DME) costing more than $500.
- Genetic testing.
- Home health care and skilled nursing facility stays.

Emergency services never require prior authorization.

## Decision Timelines
- Standard requests: a decision is made within 7 calendar days of receiving all required clinical information.
- Urgent (expedited) requests: a decision is made within 72 hours.

## Validity
An approved prior authorization is valid for 90 days from the approval date. If the service is not completed within 90 days, a new request is required.

## What Happens Without Prior Authorization
If a service that requires prior authorization is performed without approval, the claim is denied with denial code PA-01. The member may file an appeal, and the provider may request a retroactive review within 30 days of the denial.
