# Evergreen Silver HMO - Summary of Benefits (Plan Year 2026)

## Plan Overview
The Evergreen Silver HMO is a health maintenance organization plan. Members must choose a primary care physician (PCP) who coordinates care. A referral from the PCP is required before seeing a specialist. There is no out-of-network coverage except for emergency care.

## Deductibles and Out-of-Pocket Maximum
- Deductible: $2,000 individual / $4,000 family.
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $7,500 individual / $15,000 family.
- After the deductible is met, member coinsurance is 30%.

## Copays
- Primary care (PCP) office visit: $35 copay.
- Specialist office visit (with referral): $70 copay.
- Telehealth visit through Evergreen Virtual Care: $10 copay.
- Urgent care center: $75 copay.
- Emergency room: $400 copay, waived if admitted.

## Out-of-Network Care
Services from out-of-network providers are not covered on the Silver HMO, except for emergency services and urgent care received while traveling outside the service area.

## Preventive Care
Preventive care is covered at 100% with no deductible when received from an in-network provider.
