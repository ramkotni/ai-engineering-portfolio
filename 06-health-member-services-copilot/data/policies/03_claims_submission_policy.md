# Claims Submission and Processing Policy

## Who Submits Claims
In-network providers submit claims directly to Evergreen Health Plans on the member's behalf. Members only need to submit a claim themselves when they receive care from an out-of-network provider who does not bill Evergreen directly.

## Timely Filing Limits
- In-network providers must submit claims within 90 days of the date of service.
- Members submitting their own out-of-network claims must do so within 12 months of the date of service.
- Claims received after the timely filing limit are denied with denial code TF-02.

## How Members Submit a Claim
Members can submit a claim through the Evergreen member portal or by mail. A complete claim must include:
1. A completed Member Claim Form (Form EHP-CL1).
2. An itemized bill from the provider showing diagnosis and procedure codes.
3. Proof of payment, if the member already paid the provider.

## Processing Timelines
- Electronic clean claims are processed within 14 calendar days.
- Paper clean claims are processed within 30 calendar days.
- A "clean claim" is a claim with all required information and no need for additional documentation.

## Claim Status Definitions
- Received: the claim has been logged and is awaiting review.
- In Review: a claims examiner is reviewing the claim.
- Pending Information: Evergreen needs more information. The member or provider has 45 days to respond before the claim is denied.
- Approved: the claim has been approved and payment is being issued.
- Paid: payment has been sent to the provider or member. An Explanation of Benefits (EOB) is available in the portal.
- Denied: the claim was not approved. The EOB lists the denial code and reason. Members may file an appeal.

## Common Denial Codes
- PA-01: Required prior authorization was not obtained.
- TF-02: Claim submitted after the timely filing limit.
- NC-03: Service is not covered under the member's plan.
- MI-04: Missing or incomplete information.
- OON-05: Out-of-network service not covered under the member's HMO plan.
