# Evergreen Gold PPO - Summary of Benefits (Plan Year 2026)

## Plan Overview
The Evergreen Gold PPO is a preferred provider organization plan. Members may see any provider, but costs are lowest when using in-network providers. No referral is required to see a specialist on the Gold PPO plan.

## Deductibles and Out-of-Pocket Maximum
- In-network deductible: $500 individual / $1,000 family.
- Out-of-network deductible: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family.
- In-network out-of-pocket maximum: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family.
- Out-of-network out-of-pocket maximum: $9,000 individual / $18,000 family.
- After the deductible is met, the plan pays 80% in-network (member coinsurance 20%) and 60% out-of-network (member coinsurance 40%).

## Copays (In-Network)
- Primary care (PCP) office visit: $20 copay.
- Specialist office visit: $40 copay.
- Telehealth visit through Evergreen Virtual Care: $0 copay.
- Urgent care center: $50 copay.
- Emergency room: $250 copay, waived if the member is admitted to the hospital.
- Ambulance (emergency): 20% coinsurance after deductible.

## Preventive Care
Preventive care services such as annual physicals, routine immunizations, and screening mammograms are covered at 100% in-network with no deductible.

## Hospital and Surgical Services
- Inpatient hospital stay: 20% coinsurance after deductible. Non-emergency admissions require prior authorization.
- Outpatient surgery: 20% coinsurance after deductible. Requires prior authorization.

## Mental and Behavioral Health
- Outpatient therapy visit: $20 copay.
- Inpatient behavioral health: 20% coinsurance after deductible.
