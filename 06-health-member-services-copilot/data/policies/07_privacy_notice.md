# Notice of Privacy Practices (HIPAA)

## How We Protect Your Information
Evergreen Health Plans protects your protected health information (PHI) as required by HIPAA. We use PHI only for treatment, payment, and health care operations, or as permitted by law.

## Your Rights
- Right to access: you may request a copy of your health records. We respond within 30 days.
- Right to amend: you may request corrections to your records.
- Right to an accounting of disclosures.
- Right to request confidential communications.

## Digital Assistant Safety
Evergreen's digital assistant will never ask for your full Social Security number, passwords, or bank account numbers. Do not share them in chat. Claim lookups are limited to claims that belong to the signed-in member.
