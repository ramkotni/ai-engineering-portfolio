# Member Services FAQ

## How do I contact Member Services?
Call Member Services at 1-800-555-0142, Monday through Friday, 8am to 8pm Eastern Time. You can also send a secure message through the member portal.

## How do I get a replacement ID card?
Download a digital ID card from the Evergreen mobile app, or request a physical replacement card in the portal. Physical cards arrive within 7 to 10 business days.

## How do I change my primary care physician (PCP)?
Silver HMO members can change their PCP once per month through the portal. The change takes effect on the first day of the following month.

## Can I add a dependent to my plan?
Dependents can be added during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth, or adoption. Children are eligible until age 26.

## Am I covered when traveling?
Gold PPO members have in-network access nationwide through the Evergreen TravelCare network. Silver HMO members are covered only for emergency and urgent care while traveling outside the service area.

## When is open enrollment?
Open enrollment runs from November 1 through December 15 each year, with coverage effective January 1.
